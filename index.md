My name is Lihu Chen (陈立虎), and I am currently an ISFP Researcher (permanent position) at [Inria Saclay](https://www.inria.fr/fr/centre-inria-de-saclay) in the [CEDAR](https://inria.fr/en/cedar) team.
Previously, I was a Research Associate at [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/). 
Before that, I did a one-year postdoc at [Inria Saclay](https://www.inria.fr/en/inria-saclay-centre). I obtained my PhD at [DIG](https://dig.telecom-paris.fr/blog/) team of [Télécom Paris](https://www.telecom-paris.fr/en/home), which is a member of [Institut Polytechnique de Paris](https://www.ip-paris.fr/en). I was co-supervised by [Fabian Suchanek](https://suchanek.name/) and [Gaël Varoquaux](http://gael-varoquaux.info/). <br>

My research primarily focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and large language models (LLMs).
I am dedicated to developing efficient, reliable, and open-source models and tools, with a particular emphasis on information extraction and biomedical applications. Specifically, my research topics include: 

* **Large vs Small: Collaborative AI Systems (Frugal AI)**.  In our [survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.06857), we systematically examine the collaborations between LLMs and SMs.
My research will build upon this foundational concept, focusing on leveraging the complementary strengths of both LLMs and SMs. 
* **Generation-Free Signals in Foundation Models**. I am interested in using internal signals of models to make them more efficient and trustworthy. The question here is how we can find useful signals before generating any answer tokens.
* **Efficient Knowledge-Augmented LLMs**. LLMs may struggle with tasks that require domain-specific expertise or up-to-date information. I aim to explore cost-effective methods for enhancing reasoning capabilities, e.g. logical reasoning and medical applications. 
* **Long-term Research Question: Can AI Develop Human-like Reasoning Capabilities—or Go Beyond Them?**: How can we develop AI systems that go beyond statistical patterns? New Architectures? Multimodality? New Learning Objectives? Neuro-symbolic Integration? We'll see!
